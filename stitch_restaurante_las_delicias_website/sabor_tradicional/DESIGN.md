## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the warm, rooted hospitality of authentic Costa Rican culinary culture, translating the sensory spirit of a classic sodita and traditional family dining table into a contemporary digital surface. It bridges artisan culinary heritage with effortless utility, avoiding folklore pastiche in favor of earthy richness, honest ingredients, and generous welcome.

The aesthetic fuses Tactile Warmth with Modern Editorial structure. Broad canvas areas of warm cream, sunbaked terracotta, toasted earth accents, and vegetable-dyed ochres invite the guest to linger. Interfaces evoke handcrafted clay cazuelas, coarse linen napkins, and rich coffee aromas through layered tonal containers, soft border outlines, and deliberate typographic rhythm.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from Central Valley kitchens, wood-fired hearths, and tropical harvest tones:

- **Primary (`#8B263E` — Terracotta Vinotinto):** Anchors brand touchpoints, primary actions, category highlights, and active states. It delivers depth and traditional nobility without clinical aggression.
- **Secondary (`#F9F6F0` — Warm Cream):** Forms the foundational surface background, soft inner cards, and breathable gutters, providing an inviting, natural alternative to stark optical white.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` — Saffron Amber):** Reserved as the high-visibility conversion accent for call-to-action buttons, limited-run seasonal dishes, delivery triggers, and order badges.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` — Charcoal):** Used for primary editorial reading, prices, and high-legibility interface controls. Subordinate tints step down to `#4B5563` (Charcoal Muted) for secondary descriptions and `#E6DEC8` (Adobe Bone) for hairline borders and divider rules.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative heritage with rapid, frictionless ordering.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Imparts editorial polish, culinary romance, and timeless prestige. Use for menu section banners, plate titles in featured spotlights, and celebratory narrative cards.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers open counters, tall x-height, and optimal legibility in busy dining conditions and dense digital order baskets.
- Maintain loose letterforms in body paragraphs with ample line height to mirror relaxed table pacing; retain tight, compact metrics on micro-labels, modifiers, and receipt line items.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid underpins all viewports, shifting to 8 columns on tablet devices (600px–1024px) and 4 columns on mobile handsets (&lt;600px).

- **Grid Alignment:** Canvas content maximum width rests at `1280px` for desktop to prevent expansive horizontal menu drift.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a cohesive 4px baseline grid. Dish cards and informational groupings utilize `space-md` internally, with `space-xl` segregating distinct culinary categories (Entradas, Casados Tradicionales, Ollas, Postres).
- **Responsive Flow:** Mobile menus collapse category navigators into sticky horizontal carousels, locking the bottom bar for reservation and cart triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages warm ambient grounding rather than cold synthetic drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Base screen canvas in Warm Cream (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Elevated white/cream tiles (`#FFFFFF` to `#FCFAF6`) bounded by an organic 1px border in toasted linen (`rgba(139, 38, 62, 0.12)`) and an ultra-subtle warm shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(78, 42, 28, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards):** Lifted card response shifting to `0 8px 20px -3px rgba(78, 42, 28, 0.09)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Order Drawer / Sticky Triggers):** Prominent overlay surfaces using `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(45, 18, 25, 0.16)` with a backdrop tint of `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.5)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic softenings reminiscent of hand-turned ceramic earthenware and carved wood serving platters.

- Standard buttons, form controls, and cards adhere to a base radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Larger layout surfaces, dish detail modal cards, and hero photo containers employ `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Micro badges, status pills, and dietary indicators apply `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to retain distinct pill silhouettes without overwhelming surrounding geometry.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary CTA (Order / Reserve):* Solid Saffron Amber (`#F59E0B`) background with Charcoal (`#1F2937`) text, semi-bold weight, and tactile warm drop shadow.
  - *Secondary Action:* Solid Terracotta Vinotinto (`#8B263E`) with Warm Cream (`#F9F6F0`) text.
  - *Tertiary / Outline:* Warm Cream background with a 1.5px border in Terracotta Vinotinto (`#8B263E`) and matching text.
- **Dish Cards:**
  - Constructed on Level 1 elevation with soft border treatment. Imagery occupies the top half with an internal aspect ratio of 4:3, featuring warm light grading. Title uses Playfair Display (`headline-sm`), followed by localized dish notes (e.g., *frijoles tiernos, plátano maduro, picadillo del día*) in Plus Jakarta Sans (`body-sm`), ending with a prominent price lockup in Terracotta Vinotinto.
- **Chips & Filters:**
  - Rounded pill shape used for course selection and dietary tags (Vegetariano, Sin Gluten). Inactive chips utilize a Warm Cream surface with a subtle `#D6CEBE` border. Active chips transition to solid Terracotta Vinotinto with Warm Cream text.
- **Form Inputs:**
  - Clean input fields with Warm Cream background (`#FAF7F2`), inset subtle border, and Charcoal label typography. On active focus, the border transitions smoothly to Terracotta Vinotinto with a soft amber glow ring (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Custom rounded checkbox controls with Terracotta Vinotinto check fills on selection, designed for selecting side accompaniments (e.g., choice of picadillo, salsa Lizano, tortas caseras).
- **Specialty Components:**
  - *Daily Special Banner ("Plato del Día"):* Textured rustic badge featuring amber accent border and Playfair Display title.
  - *Table Reservation Drawer:* Slide-out sheet on Level 3 depth featuring quick-select party size chips, date pickers, and WhatsApp direct confirmation triggers.
